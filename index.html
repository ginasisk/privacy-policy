This Privacy Policy describes how this application handles information when interacting with Pinterest.

This application may access Pinterest account information necessary to provide its functionality, including Pinterest boards, Pins, and information required to create and publish Pins through the Pinterest API.

Information accessed through Pinterest is used only to provide the application's intended functionality, including creating, managing, and publishing Pinterest content authorized by the user.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information obtained through Pinterest is not shared with third parties except as necessary to provide the application's functionality or comply with applicable law.

Data Retention and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by the application. Information is retained only as necessary to operate the application and provide its functionality.

User Control

Users may revoke the application's access to their Pinterest account through their Pinterest account settings.
